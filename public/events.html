Rules and Regulations

TECHNICAL EVENTS

IT MANAGER

[FORMAT: SOLO]
RULES & REGULATIONS:
  • Each team must consist of 1 participant.
  • The competition will consist of three rounds: Round 1 - Aptitude Test, Round 2 - On-the-spot preparation, Round 3 - Interview Round.
  • Both team members must participate in all applicable rounds.
  • Participants must report to the venue before the scheduled time.
  • The aptitude test will be conducted within the specified time limit.
  • For the on-the-spot preparation round, the topic/task will be given at the venue, and participants must complete it within the given time.
  • The interview round will assess the participants' communication, decision-making, problem-solving, and managerial skills.
  • Mobile phones or other electronic devices may not be used during the rounds unless permitted by the organizers.
  • Any form of unfair means or misconduct will lead to disqualification.
  • The judges' and organizers' decisions will be final and binding.
  • VENUE MINI AUDI
  • COORDINATORS SANIYA, ANANDHU

IT QUIZ

[FORMAT: DUO]
RULES & REGULATIONS:
  • Each team must consist of 2 participants only.
  • Only one team can participate from each participating group.
  • The quiz will consist of a question-and-answer session.
  • Questions will be asked by the quizmaster, and teams must answer within the given time limit.
  • Both team members are allowed to participate in answering the questions.
  • Participants must not use mobile phones, internet, or any external assistance during the quiz.

WEB AI

[FORMAT: TRIO]
RULES & REGULATIONS:
  • A topic/problem statement will be given to each team.
  • Participants must create a website based on the given topic using an AI tool.
  • The website should be completed within the given time limit.
  • Participants may use AI tools for generating code, design ideas, and debugging.
  • The final website will be evaluated based on creativity, design, functionality, relevance to the topic, and effective use of AI.
  • Team size: 3 members.
  • Participants must present/demo their completed website at the end.
  • VENUE BIG LAB
  • COORDINATORS SAHIL

POSTER DESIGNING

[FORMAT: SOLO]
RULES & REGULATIONS:
  • Participants must design a poster using Canva.
  • The topic/theme will be given on the spot.
  • Each participating group can register a maximum of 2 teams.
  • Participants must complete and submit the poster within the given time.
  • Only Canva may be used for designing.
  • The poster must be original, creative, and relevant to the given topic.
  • Pre-made designs should not be submitted as the final poster.
  • Judging criteria: Creativity, originality, relevance, visual appeal, and effective use of Canva.
  • VENUE SMALL LAB
  • COORDINATORS SAHANA, SUMAN
